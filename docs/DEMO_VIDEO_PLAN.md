# Hackathon Demo Video Plan

## Recommended format

- **Length:** 2 minutes 15 seconds to 2 minutes 45 seconds
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape, ideally 1920×1080 recording of the production build
- **Approach:** Mostly uninterrupted real gameplay with concise voice-over or captions; avoid long developer-tool or code footage
- **Audio:** Let one short Pan Jam pattern be heard. Keep narration clear and do not add copyrighted background music.

## Storyboard and narration guide

| Time | Footage | Suggested message |
|---:|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Title screen, then Start | “Young Folk Story Time is a child-friendly Caribbean narrative game where creativity, rhythm, and empathy shape a Carnival adventure.” |
| 0:10–0:28 | CC Club introduction and the three opening choices | Explain that the player can follow Junior, try Angel's shortcut, or work together, and that the story remembers the decision. |
| 0:28–0:45 | Costume Challenge card placement and Creator Badge | Show one or two real placements, then a short cut to completion. Mention large controls, gentle retries, and visual hints. |
| 0:45–0:55 | Story Pot transition and Carnival arrival | Let the visual change establish the journey; keep narration minimal. |
| 0:55–1:18 | Pan Jam demonstration and Rhythm Star | Play a real short pattern with sound. Point out symbols, numbers, colour-independent labels, visual feedback, and generated browser audio. |
| 1:18–1:38 | Moko Jumbie reveal and emotional choice | Show the Moko Jumbies and Angel stepping back. Explain that trust affects how openly she responds and that the player can respect her pace. |
| 1:38–1:58 | Shortcut Carnival Crisis, four choices, then safety-clip repair | Connect the crisis to the earlier shortcut. Show the real three-step fastening interaction and emphasize repair rather than punishment. |
| 1:58–2:17 | **We Fixed It** or **Together on the Road** ending | Show a short exchange that demonstrates emotional payoff from prior choices. |
| 2:17–2:32 | Final Story Card and Play Again | Point out remembered moments, the Creator Badge, Rhythm Star, ending badge, and replay. |
| 2:32–2:42 | Closing title or Story Card | “Built with Phaser and typed, testable narrative systems—no backend, account, analytics, or paid service required.” |

## Recording route

For the clearest cause-and-effect demo, use this real route:

1. Follow Angel at the CC Club.
2. Complete the costume steps in order.
3. Finish Pan Jam.
4. Ask Angel what is making her uncomfortable.
5. During the shortcut crisis, choose **Angel, tell me what happened**.
6. Complete the safety-clip repair to reach **We Fixed It**.

Record a second short insert of **Work Together** and the non-shortcut crisis only if time permits; do not imply both crises occur in one run.

## Production checklist

- Record `pnpm preview`, not the development server.
- Confirm 1280×720 or a larger 16:9 viewport, sound on, normal motion, no browser console errors, and no desktop notifications.
- Capture actual pointer or keyboard input and retain the game's visible instructions.
- Use straight cuts to remove waiting time; do not stage fake choices, modify story state, or replace screenshots with generated art.
- Add captions for key narration and do not rely on game audio alone.
- Credit the creator-supplied Young Folk artwork and list Phaser, TypeScript, Vite, and Vitest in the closing card or video description.
- Export locally with a free/open-source tool such as OBS Studio and Shotcut or Kdenlive. Do not activate a paid trial or upload automatically.
